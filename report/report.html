How the n+1 results will be checked

The predictions above are frozen in outputs/predictions_nplus1.csv with their origin date. After the next trading session, tools/check_predictions.py fetches the realised closes, computes the actual next-day returns, and scores direction accuracy and MAE against the frozen file — the same discipline as the walk-forward evaluation, applied to the live forecast.

Methods & limitations (summary)

Features use price history and daily aggregated social/news sentiment only; targets are next-trading-day returns. Validation is an expanding-window walk-forward — no shuffling, no lookahead. Social sentiment history is limited to the last 10 days by the assignment's design, so most training rows carry the coverage-flagged neutral treatment; lexicon scoring for Chinese/Korean forum text captures directional market vocabulary but not sarcasm or negation. Full detail in the repository README. This is a research prototype, not financial advice.
